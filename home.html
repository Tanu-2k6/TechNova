What is TechNova?

TechNova is a simple personal finance management platform designed to help you make better financial decisions. Instead of keeping track of your finances manually.TechNova brings important financial information together in one place.

What Can You Do With TechNova?

💸 Expense Tracker

Record your daily expenses and understand where your money is being spent.

🎯 Goal Planner

Set financial goals and calculate how much you need to save every month.

📊 Risk Assessment

Understand your investment risk profile based on your investment duration and risk tolerance.

❤️ Financial Health

Get a simple financial health score based on your savings and spending habits.

💡 Financial Insights

Receive useful insights that can help you improve your financial habits.
